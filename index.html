Personal automation tools

This page describes two small OAuth clients used privately by a single person (Kyung min Goh). They are not public services, and no one else can sign up for them.

  • md2video: uploads review videos that the owner made to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • rclone-server: lets the owner's own server copy files to and from the owner's own Google Drive using rclone.
