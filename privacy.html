Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy covers the OAuth clients md2video and rclone-server. Both are personal tools used only by their owner, Kyung min Goh, with the owner's own Google account.

What data is accessed

  • md2video: permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • rclone-server: access to files in the owner's Google Drive.

How it is used

The data is used only to perform the owner's own uploads and file copies. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own laptop and server. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
